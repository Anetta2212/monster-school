      School Rules

      Read them before the bell rings. Nobody has ever read them after.

      1. Coffins

        No sleeping in coffins during lessons.

      2. Potions

        Never drink a potion without permission.

      3. Fridays

        Every student must scare the headteacher on Fridays.

      4. Corridors

        No flying on broomsticks in the corridors.

      5. Manners

        No biting your classmates.

      6. Tests

        Ghosts must not disappear during tests.

      7. Frogs

        No turning teachers into frogs.

      8. Lessons

        Werewolves must not howl during lessons.

      Break the rules and face the Headmaster. Detention is in the cellar, with Professor Whisper.
