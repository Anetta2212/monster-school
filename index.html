      School Rules

      Read them before the bell rings. Nobody has ever read them after.

      1. Dress code

        Black, grey or deep purple only. Bright colours attract attention, and attention is dangerous.

      2. The bell

        Lessons begin when the bell rings. If you cannot find the bell, follow the howling.

      3. Classroom 13

        Do not open the door of Classroom 13. It is not on the school map. That is the point.

      4. Corridors

        No running. Floating, flying and gliding are allowed, but keep to the right.

      5. Homework

        Hand it in on time. “A ghost ate it” is not an excuse, because ghosts do not eat.

      6. Respect

        Be kind to every student, whether they have fur, fangs, scales or no body at all.

      7. Tests

        No spells, bites or roaring during an exam. Peeking through the wall counts as cheating.

      8. The library

        Silence, please. Only ghosts may whisper, because they cannot help it.

      9. Portraits

        Never turn your back on the portraits. They gossip.

      10. Visitors

        Every visitor must pass the entrance exam. If you are reading this, you already did.

      Breaking a rule means detention in the cellar, with Professor Whisper.
